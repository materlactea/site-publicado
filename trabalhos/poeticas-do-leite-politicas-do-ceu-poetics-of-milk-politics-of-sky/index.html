poetics of milk, politics of sky, 2021
https://ceciliacavalieri.github.io/nebulx/

poetics of milk, politics of sky is a cosmogonic journey that begins in one dream and ends in another. between these two points, an essay composed-with mammæls discusses micro and macrocosmic issues of gender, nutrition, language and care.

made-with: web enablement by luisa burini [https://github.com/luisaburini], voices-over dora cavalieri penna & joice marino [portuguese], antonia toledo de morales & cécile morel [french], ciana lago & tâmara jones [english]. lactose constellation, cosmic latte #fff8e7 johns hopkins university, breast milk under the microscope, william herschel's milky way projected on the wall, poem-report ultrasound embryo, milkmoon [monika & leo], anonymous doe’s milk moon, anonymous cow's milk moon, wheat flour from the small farmers movement, davi kopenawa, wikipedia, levanha stone, three year old milk cups, poem-report gallbladder stone, seahorse in labor, anonymous ewe's milk moon, active levanha, calcium salt, bilirubin and cholesterol stone, laranja ribas, beatriz toledo, drosophila larvae on levanha egg gallstone shapped, anonymous co2 alveoli in the microscope, english and french proofreading by joão camillo penna, anonymous sour cow's milk under the microscope, sound of hydrogen, natalia quinderé’s milk stone.
